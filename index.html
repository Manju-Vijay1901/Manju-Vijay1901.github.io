Projects

Telecom Incident & SLA Dashboard

Tool: Microsoft Excel 2021

Project type: Data analysis and reporting

Analysed 20 telecom incident records to evaluate incident priorities, response and resolution times, and Service Level Agreement (SLA) performance.

Key Findings

20 Total Incidents
19 Closed Incidents
1 Open Incident
63.16% SLA Compliance
7 SLA Breaches
4 Critical Incidents

Business Insights

  • Reviewed 20 incidents across multiple cities and teams.
  • 12 of 19 closed incidents met their SLA targets.
  • 7 closed incidents exceeded their SLA targets.
  • Critical incidents require appropriate prioritisation.
Business Recommendation

Investigate the seven SLA breaches to identify recurring causes, prioritise critical incidents, and find opportunities to improve resolution performance.

Note: This project uses practice data created for portfolio learning.
